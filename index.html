Dear Oumaima,

There are people we meet and remember.

Then there are people who quietly become part of our lives until we can't imagine our story without them.

Eight years ago, I didn't know that one day you would become one of the most important people in my life.

We survived high school together. We survived prepa together. We shared meals, memories, laughter, stress, and countless ordinary moments that somehow became some of my favorite memories.

I still laugh when I remember us running after buses we never managed to catch. I still smile thinking about how I would always complain that you took forever to get ready, only to wait for you every single time.

And somehow, even after going to different schools, we stayed us.

Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every piece of advice, every moment of comfort, and every memory we've created together.

I love your honey-olive eyes, your dimply smile, your warm presence, and most of all the person behind them.

Thank you for being my best friend.

Happy 23rd Birthday.

I love you and I am endlessly grateful for you.

Forever your friend 💙
